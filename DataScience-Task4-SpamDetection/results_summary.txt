Why Recall Matters for Spam Detection:
- Recall = TP / (TP + FN)
- High recall ensures most spam messages are caught
- False Negatives (spam in inbox) are more harmful than False Positives (ham in spam folder)

Key Insights:
1. Text preprocessing (lowercase, remove punctuation, remove stopwords, stemming) improves model performance
2. TF-IDF effectively captures important words for classification
3. Common spam words: "free", "win", "cash", "prize", "urgent", "claim", "discount"
4. Common ham words: "ok", "home", "call", "time", "love", "good", "happy"
